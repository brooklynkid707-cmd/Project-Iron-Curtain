# Defense-in-Depth

Project Iron Curtain uses preventive, detective, and corrective controls to create multiple layers of security.

## Preventive

Controls intended to reduce the likelihood of successful compromise include:

- Endpoint protection
- Email filtering
- Network perimeter controls
- Multi-Factor Authentication
- Data protection controls

## Detective

Controls intended to identify suspicious or malicious activity include:

- SIEM monitoring
- Centralized telemetry
- Correlation rules
- SOC analysis
- Threat intelligence

## Corrective

Controls supporting response and recovery include:

- Incident response
- Investigation
- Containment
- Evidence preservation
- Recovery procedures

## Design Philosophy

No individual control provides complete protection.

The architecture combines controls across endpoints, networks, identity systems, cloud environments, and organizational processes so failure of one layer does not eliminate the organization's ability to detect or respond to an attack.
