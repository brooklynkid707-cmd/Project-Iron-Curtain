# Cybersecurity Strategy

## Strategic Objective

Project Iron Curtain proposes moving the case organization from reactive security practices toward a proactive, risk-driven security operations model.

## Strategy

The program follows this lifecycle:

Asset Identification
        |
        v
Risk Identification
        |
        v
Likelihood and Impact Assessment
        |
        v
Risk Prioritization
        |
        v
Risk-to-Control Mapping
        |
        v
Defense-in-Depth Architecture
        |
        v
Centralized Monitoring
        |
        v
SOC Operations
        |
        v
Incident Response
        |
        v
Continuous Improvement

## Business Alignment

Cybersecurity is treated as a business-risk management discipline supporting:

- Operational continuity
- Protection of intellectual property
- Protection of sensitive information
- Organizational resilience
- Governance and accountability

## Control Strategy

Security technologies should be selected according to the risks they mitigate.

The architecture combines endpoint, email, network, identity, data-protection, monitoring, and response capabilities.

## Operational Strategy

Centralized SIEM monitoring provides visibility across otherwise isolated technology systems.

SOC processes convert telemetry and alerts into operational investigation and response.

## Scalability

The proposed architecture is intended to provide a security foundation that can evolve as the organization grows and its threat environment changes.
