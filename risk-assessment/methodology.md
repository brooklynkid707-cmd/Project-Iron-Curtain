# Risk Assessment Methodology

## Approach

Project Iron Curtain uses an asset-based qualitative risk assessment.

The assessment evaluates:

1. Critical organizational assets
2. Relevant cyber threats
3. Existing vulnerabilities
4. Likelihood of exploitation
5. Potential business impact
6. Resulting risk priority

## Assets Considered

The assessment included:

- Employee endpoints
- Servers
- Network infrastructure
- Cloud storage
- Mobile devices
- Proprietary software
- CRM systems
- Development documentation
- Financial systems

## Risk Prioritization

Likelihood and impact were evaluated qualitatively using Medium and High classifications.

Resulting risks were classified as Medium, High, or Critical.

The objective is not simply to produce a risk score.

The assessment provides a business-driven foundation for selecting controls that address the organization's highest exposures first.

## Security Principle

Security investment should follow risk.

Technology should be selected because it mitigates an identified organizational risk rather than because the technology is popular or widely deployed.
