# Enterprise Risk Register

The Project Iron Curtain assessment used a qualitative model based on asset, threat, vulnerability, likelihood, and business impact.

| ID | Asset | Threat | Vulnerability | Likelihood | Impact | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Employee laptops | Malware infection | Unrestricted software downloads | High | High | Critical |
| R2 | Corporate email | Phishing attack | Lack of email filtering | High | High | Critical |
| R3 | Cloud storage | Data breach | Weak access controls | Medium | High | High |
| R4 | Routers | Unauthorized access | Default configurations | Medium | High | High |
| R5 | Proprietary software | Intellectual property theft | Lack of monitoring | High | High | Critical |
| R6 | Mobile devices | Device loss | No mobile device management | Medium | Medium | Medium |
| R7 | Servers | Ransomware | Unpatched systems | High | High | Critical |
| R8 | CRM software | Data leakage | Excessive permissions | Medium | High | High |
| R9 | Development documentation | Insider threat | Lack of access auditing | Medium | High | High |
| R10 | Financial systems | Fraudulent transactions | Weak authentication | Medium | High | High |

## Priority Findings

The assessment identified malware propagation, phishing-based fraud, intellectual property theft, and ransomware as the most severe risks.

These findings provide the basis for control selection and security investment prioritization.
